How to play Spellunk

Guess four 5-letter words that form a square. The corners are shared between neighboring words and the answers are all loosely connected by a similar theme.

  • Top reads left → right
  • Right reads top → bottom
  • Bottom reads left → right
  • Left reads top → bottom

Tap a side of the square, type a guess, and press Enter. You start with 8 guesses. Solve a side and you earn 2 more.

A new square is available each day.

Colors work like this:

R

Green = correct letter & position
Yellow = letter is in the word but wrong spot
Black = letter not in that word
